## Detailed Notes

### 2026-01-20 — Customer observation: Operations lead (wholesale distributor)

**Track:** solo

**Context:** Informal conversation with the operations lead at a small wholesale distributor (roughly 20 staff) about how purchase orders move through the business today.

#### What they said
- Roughly half their week is spent reading supplier emails and PDFs, then copying line items into a spreadsheet by hand.
- Their biggest fear is a fat-fingered quantity that turns into a wrong delivery, which then costs a day of phone calls to unwind.
- They have looked at general accounting tools but none of them ingest the messy supplier documents they actually receive.

#### Why it matters
- This is buyer-side demand for the exact wedge OrderFlow targets: turning supplier-document chaos into one approvable order queue.
- It is one conversation, not a validated market. The next step is a paid pilot to convert an interested operator into a real paid signal, which is the difference between a Validating idea and an Active one.

#### Open questions
- Would they pay per seat, and at what number, once the spreadsheet pain is removed?
- How messy is the worst supplier format they receive, and does document parsing hold up against it?
