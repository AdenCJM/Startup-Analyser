# Runway & Sequencing Framework

**Purpose:** sequence your portfolio when you have finite runway and no salary backstop. This framework answers "can I start this now, does it use my edge, and can it reach revenue inside my runway?" It does not change *what* is worth building. It changes *when* and *in what order* you reach for each idea.

It sits alongside the other lenses:

- **Founder Constraints** answers "can I build this, given who I am?"
- **Market Opportunity** answers "is this worth building, regardless of founder?"
- **Runway & Sequencing** answers "can I start this now, does it use my edge, and can it reach revenue within my runway?"

The other lenses converge on *what* the best bet is. This one decides *what order* you swing in, and whether a given idea is reachable from where your cash sits today.

---

## The Tarzan principle

Never let go of one vine until the next is bearing your weight.

When you are funding yourself, the runway itself is the vine you are holding. It is the cash that keeps you airborne. The discipline is simple: do not grab a vine that cannot bear weight before your runway runs out. Sequence so each bet reaches a revenue or fundable milestone before the runway funding it is exhausted.

The corollary: an idea that cannot start until some prior condition is met (cash flow, domain knowledge, a co-founder, a relationship you do not yet have) is a *Next Vine*, not a *Current Vine*. You reach it after the current one bears weight.

## The three filters

Score each PASS / Mixed / FAIL. Justify in one line.

### 1. Startable Now

Can you begin meaningful work on this immediately, given your current focus, skills, and attention?

| Score | Definition |
|---|---|
| PASS | You can begin real work today with current skills and focus. No blocking prerequisite: no co-founder still to recruit, no domain to learn from scratch, no gate to clear before any progress is possible. |
| Mixed | You can start, but a meaningful component is blocked on something not yet in hand: a term to settle, a capability to stand up, a relationship to open. Progress is possible but partial. |
| FAIL | You cannot meaningfully begin until a hard prerequisite is resolved: a co-founder not yet committed, capital not yet raised for a capital-first build, a long certification before first revenue. |

**Ask:** if you cleared your calendar this week and pointed all your focus at this, could you make real progress, or are you blocked on something you do not yet have?

### 2. Capability Leverage

Does this deploy the rare, valuable skills you have already built?

| Score | Definition |
|---|---|
| PASS | Directly deploys existing expertise, connections, or reputation. You are working from a position of strength. Career capital is being spent, not accumulated from scratch. |
| Mixed | Some overlap with existing skills, but significant new domain knowledge is required. Partial transfer of career capital. |
| FAIL | Starting from zero in an unfamiliar domain. No existing expertise, connections, or reputation to lean on. Career capital must be built before it can be spent. |

**Ask:** does your specific, hard-won edge give you a real right-to-win here, or are you just another newcomer? The point is to spend the career capital you already have, not to throw it away by starting fresh in an unrelated field.

### 3. Runway Fit

Can this idea reach revenue, or a clean fundable milestone, within your available runway?

| Score | Definition |
|---|---|
| PASS | A realistic path to first revenue or a fundable milestone sits comfortably inside the current runway. Self-funded runway covers a build-to-revenue cycle, or the idea is fundable now on your edge plus early traction. |
| Mixed | Reachable inside runway but tight, or dependent on a step-change that is not yet committed (a raise, an enterprise deal that may slip). Sequencing risk is real. |
| FAIL | Cannot plausibly reach revenue or a fundable milestone inside the runway: capital-first economics, a long sales cycle with no early wedge, or a market where revenue arrives only after the runway is gone. |

**Ask:** counting from today's cash position, does first revenue (or the next raise) land before the runway runs out? Restated as the Tarzan test: do not bet the runway on a vine that bears weight only after it snaps.

## Sequence Position

Beyond the three filters, place every idea in the vine-to-vine sequence. This is separate from the verdict.

| Position | Definition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Current Vine** | The immediate bet to build now. Must pass all three filters. | Build now. |
| **Next Vine** | Requires the current vine to succeed first (cash flow, domain knowledge, a co-founder, a relationship). | Watch and prepare. |
| **Career Capital Builder** | Not a vine in itself, but it strengthens your grip on the next one. It can also fill the income-bridge slot alongside the primary bet. | Run alongside the current vine, with bounded focus. |
| **Not Sequenceable** | Incompatible with your current sequencing strategy, or already killed or parked for other reasons. | Ignore unless circumstances change. |

The sequence is not decoration. It enforces the Tarzan discipline against a finite runway. You can only swing to one vine at a time.

---

## The verdict

Unlike the scored lenses, Runway & Sequencing does not produce a number. It produces a **verdict**:

| Verdict | Criteria | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **SWING** | All three filters PASS | Startable now, uses your edge, reaches revenue inside runway. Build it. |
| **STRETCH** | Two filters PASS, one Mixed | Reachable but needs careful planning. Identify the Mixed filter and plan around it. |
| **WAIT** | One or more filters FAIL | Not sequenceable today. May become SWING if circumstances change. Document what would need to change. |

## How this lens interacts with the others

Runway & Sequencing is the sequencing layer on top of the other frameworks. It does not change what is worth building, it changes when and in what order you reach for it.

A single idea can be:

- **BUILD + SWING.** Start now.
- **BUILD + WAIT.** A good idea at the wrong time. It needs the current vine to succeed first.
- **OPPORTUNITY GAP + WAIT.** A big market, but doubly blocked: wrong founder fit and wrong sequence.
- **PASS + SWING.** Startable today and a fit, but the market ceiling is too small to be the primary bet. A candidate income bridge to run alongside it instead.
- **KILL + anything.** Dead regardless of sequencing.

The key use: **Runway & Sequencing resolves ties between BUILD ideas.** When several ideas pass your constraints, the one that is also SWING beats the one that is STRETCH, which beats the one that is WAIT.

## The Career Capital Flywheel

The deepest reason sequencing matters: mastery comes before the big mission, not after it. You get so good at building that the right opportunities become visible from the cutting edge, opportunities that were invisible from the starting line.

The flywheel turns like this:

1. **Build the primary bet.** You gain specific knowledge in the domain and deepen your edge.
2. **Run an income-bridge builder alongside it.** You codify and share what you learn, extend runway, and build reputation.
3. **Career capital accumulates.** New opportunities become visible that you could not see before.
4. **Next vines become reachable.** Constraint-flip conditions get met through traction and runway, not through waiting.

The big mission becomes actionable from the cutting edge of a field, and the cutting edge is reached by building, not by deliberating.
