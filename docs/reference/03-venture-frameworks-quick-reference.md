# Venture Frameworks Quick Reference

One-page summary of the venture track's four lenses and the master verdict cascade. For detailed scoring guidance, see `ventures/reference/`.

## Venture Track Overview

The venture track evaluates **VC-backed, co-founded, full-time ideas** under assumptions opposite to the solo track. Capital is required, the leap is full-time, the target is a fund-returning exit, and the partnership is scored as a first-class axis.

**Do not run both the solo six and the venture seven on one idea.** They encode opposite conditions and produce contradictory scores.

---

## The Four Lenses

### 1. Venture Constraints (7 filters)

Answers: **Can we build this as a VC-backed, full-time, co-founded venture?**

**Scoring:** Each constraint is PASS / Mixed / FAIL.  
**Result:** Count PASS results. **Founder Fit = High if ≥5 PASS, Low if <5 PASS.**

| Constraint | PASS means | FAIL means |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Sustained Zone-of-Genius** | Your half (product, domain, customers) energises you; draining surface (raise, org, board) lands on co-founder | The work the company needs from you is draining, regardless of the title |
| 2. **Model Curve** (near-gate) | Value compounds with frontier models; sits in a model-proof layer (data, position, integration) | Core value is a wrapper around frontier models; commoditised by each release |
| 3. **Venture-Scale Outcome** (near-gate) | Company can return a fund AND your post-dilution slice clears your number | Company cannot reach fund scale, OR your slice is junior and only clears in the best case |
| 4. **Fundability & Capital Strategy** | A venture-fundable shape (not services/consulting/body-shop); investors would back it | Services business, no venture-scale path, or unit economics that never justify dilution |
| 5. **Motivation & Conviction Fuel** (hard gate) | Why you are here is mission + positive-sum value; fuel survives year 3 | Here for the status seat / title / co-founder halo; predictably burns out by year 3 |
| 6. **Conviction & Opportunity Cost** | Conviction in this idea exceeds the cost of your current path; the conviction is your own | Conviction does not beat the opportunity cost, or it is borrowed from the co-founder |
| 7. **Full-Time Commitment & Personal Runway** (hard gate) | You can credibly go full-time and survive personally for years: salary covers costs, runway covers gaps | Founder salary will not cover costs, runway is insufficient, or dependants make the leap untenable |

**Gate types:**
- **Hard gates (5, 7):** A FAIL caps the Master Verdict before the 2x2. No market opportunity rescues a motivation FAIL or a personal-runway FAIL.
- **Near-gates (2, 3):** A FAIL is near-fatal but not an automatic cap. The cascade may surface a reshape.
- **Tolerance (1, 4, 6):** Feed the X/7 PASS tally that sets the founder-fit axis.

---

### 2. Market Opportunity (5 dimensions, venture-scale)

Answers: **Is this worth building, judged at venture scale?**

**Scoring:** Each dimension is 1–5. Composite = simple average of five.  
**Bands:** ≥4.0 HIGH / 2.5–3.9 MED / <2.5 LOW.

| Dimension | 1 (weak) | 3 (moderate) | 5 (strong) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Problem Severity** | Nice-to-have; customers live with it | Real pain; would consider paying | Makes customers lose money; urgent fix |
| **Market Size** | Hundreds world-wide (sub-venture scale) | Thousands to tens of thousands in region | Millions in region; venture-scale TAM |
| **Defensibility** | Commodity; easily copied | Some switching cost or data moat | Real network effect, regulatory position, or data moat |
| **Timing** | Technology doesn't exist yet | Technology proven; market starting to notice | Market actively looking; category heating |
| **Value Capture** | Customers won't pay even if value exists | Customers might pay if positioned right | Customers see value and pay willingly |

---

### 3. Partnership Fit (6 weighted dimensions)

Answers: **Should you build this *with this co-founder*, and on what terms?**

**Scoring:** Each dimension is 1–5. Multiply by weight, sum for composite.  
**Composite:** 1.0 to 5.0 weighted scale.  
**Gate bands:** Strong (≥4.5) / Workable (3.5–4.4) / Fragile (2.5–3.4) / Misaligned (<2.5).

**Where to validate:** Any co-founder claim that loads a score (investor network, reputation, prior wins) must be validated from primary sources before recording final scores. Mark provisional scores until validated.

| Dimension | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Roles & Complementarity | 20% | Do the agreed roles cover product + commercial + capital without overlap or gap? |
| Combined Founder-Market Fit | 20% | Right-to-win: the product of both founders' stacks on this specific idea (scored HERE only) |
| Equity & Commitment Alignment | 20% | Is the split fair to contribution? Is commitment symmetric? |
| Investor Access as an Asset | 15% | Does the co-founder's network materially de-risk the raise? (provisional until validated) |
| Trust & Working History | 15% | Depth and recency of shared working history? Have you shipped together under pressure? |
| Break Conditions / Founder Pre-nup | 10% | Are vesting, control, walk-away terms agreed before the leap, not after? |

---

### 4. Venture Feasibility (4 filters + Ethics/Legal veto)

Answers: **Can this get funded, legal, built, and sold within the founders' ethical line?**

**Scoring:** PASS / Mixed / FAIL, except Ethics/Legal which is binary PASS/FAIL (a VETO).  
**Composite:** CLEARED / CONDITIONAL / BLOCKED.

| Filter | Type | What it scores |
|---|---|---|
| **Capital Path** | Gating | Credible seed lead + a de-risking milestone + runway to next round + non-dilutive overlay. A Mixed here forces CONDITIONAL. |
| **Go-to-Market / Regulatory Pathway** | Gating | Credible path to first customers (or regulatory approval if required). A Mixed here forces CONDITIONAL. |
| **Build & Operate** | Non-gating | Realistically buildable and operable by this team, in this timeframe, given the capital plan. |
| **Ethics / Legal** | Veto (binary) | The product does not cross ethical lines AND is legally defensible. A FAIL here forces Master Verdict = NO-GO. |

## The Master Verdict Cascade

Every venture resolves to **one label**, determined by a six-step cascade:

### Step 1: Hard-gate check (Constraints 5 & 7)
- **If either hard gate is FAIL:** verdict = PASS or NO-GO (depending on whether Ethics is also FAIL). **Stop here.**

### Step 2: Ethics/Legal veto (Feasibility)
- **If Ethics/Legal is FAIL:** verdict = NO-GO. **Stop here.**

### Step 3: Feasibility composite
- **If BLOCKED:** verdict = NO-GO.
- **If CONDITIONAL:** proceed to step 4.
- **If CLEARED:** proceed to step 4.

### Step 4: Market band (Market Opportunity)
- **If LOW (<2.5):** verdict = PASS.
- **If MED or HIGH:** proceed to step 5.

### Step 5: Founder fit (Constraints X/7 PASS)
- **If LOW (<5 PASS) and Market is MED:** verdict = PASS.
- **If HIGH (5+ PASS):** proceed to step 6.

### Step 6: Partnership gate & equity-protection modulator
- **If Partnership is Misaligned:** verdict = PASS.
- **If Partnership is Fragile (AND Feasibility is CONDITIONAL):** verdict = SHAPE (blocker: partnership).
- **If Venture-Scale Outcome (Constraint 3) is Mixed on sub-check (b) — post-dilution slice is soft — AND partnership is Fragile or Fragile-to-Workable:** modulator pulls verdict down toward SHAPE (blocker: equity protection).
- **Otherwise, derive final verdict:**
  - Market HIGH + Founder HIGH + Partnership Strong/Workable + Feasibility CLEARED → **PURSUE**
  - Market HIGH + Founder HIGH + Partnership or Feasibility not fully cleared → **SHAPE**
  - Market HIGH + (Founder LOW or Feasibility CONDITIONAL) → **WATCH** (or PASS if partnership misaligned)
  - Market MED + Founder HIGH + Partnership Strong → **PURSUE**
  - Market MED + others → **WATCH** or **PASS**

---

## Master Verdicts Defined

| Verdict | Meaning | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PURSUE** | Right founders, real market, feasible, partnership holds. Build now. | Active (max one) | Advance immediately. This is the one venture active at a time. |
| **SHAPE** | Real opportunity with a named blocker to resolve (founder-fit, market, feasibility, cap table, partnership). | Shaping | Work the blocker. Re-score when it is resolved. Typical early co-founding state. |
| **WATCH** | Market is real (≥4.0) and Ethics passes, but venture conditions are not yet met. Document Flip Conditions. | Watching | Monitor signals. Review quarterly. Highest-market WATCH idea is the Crown Jewel. |
| **PASS** | Does not clear the lenses under this condition, or good-fit-but-sub-scale (route to solo track). | Backlog | Does not merit venture-track focus now. |
| **NO-GO** | Vetoed: Ethics/Legal FAIL or Feasibility BLOCKED. Terminal; never re-surfaced silently. | Killed | Hard terminal. Record the reason in the README post-mortem. |

## Lifecycle Statuses (Venture Track)

```
Backlog → Validating → Active (one max)
   ↓ ↓ ↓
Shaping (named blocker being resolved)
   ↓
Parked / Watching (off-ramps)
   ↓
Killed
```

**Status meaning:**
- **Backlog:** Scored, not yet validating.
- **Validating:** Gathering evidence (co-founder conversations, market validation, feasibility de-risking).
- **Shaping:** A SHAPE verdict: working the specific blocker (partnership, cap table, founder-fit, market clarity, feasibility condition).
- **Active:** A PURSUE verdict; the one venture being built full-time. At most one venture Active at a time.
- **Watching:** A WATCH verdict; high-market idea held for quarterly review of Flip Conditions.
- **Parked:** Viable but deprioritised.
- **Killed:** A PASS or NO-GO verdict; off-ramp terminal.

---

## Allocation Rule

At any time, run:

- **One primary bet** — solo or venture track (Validating, Shaping, or Active)
- **One income-bridge slot** — separate, for runway extension (optional)
- **Everything else in Backlog**

Only one venture is Active at a time. If you are pursuing a venture at PURSUE, the solo track is in Backlog.

## Quick comparison: Venture vs Solo Constraints

| Dimension | Venture Constraint | Solo Constraint | Relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder fit | Zone-of-Genius (yours only; co-founder covered elsewhere) | Energy, not drainage | Reframed for role split |
| Model Curve | Yes; near-gate; model-proof layers required | Yes | Kept, re-anchored to venture moats |
| Exit/Scale | Venture-Scale Outcome (fund-return + your slice) | Win and exit (sellable in 5-7 years) | Inverted for exit multiples |
| Capital | Fundability: must be venture-fundable shape | Bootstrap by default: avoid capital | Inverted; capital is required |
| Motivation | Motivation & Conviction Fuel (hard gate) | Not scored separately | New; venture-specific |
| Revenue | Revenue sub-check in Venture-Scale Outcome (equity slice) | Revenue decoupled from time | Reframed as equity, not hourly |
| Personal | Full-Time Commitment & Personal Runway (hard gate) | Not scored separately | New; venture-specific (founder-salary survivability) |
| Status games | Embedded in Conviction & Opportunity Cost | Wealth games, not status | Reframed as "conviction is your own" |

## Quick checklist: Is this a venture idea or a solo idea?

| Question | Venture if yes | Solo if no |
|---|---|---|
| Am I raising institutional capital? | YES | no |
| Is the leap full-time from day one? | YES | no |
| Is there a co-founder I'm scoring with? | YES | no |
| Is the target exit / outcome a fund-returning multiple? | YES | no |
| Do I need capital before product-market fit? | YES | no |

If even one is YES, use the venture track and its seven constraints. If all are NO, use the solo track.
