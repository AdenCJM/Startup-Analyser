# Frameworks Quick Reference

The Startup Analyser system uses four lenses to evaluate ideas. This reference lists all lenses, their scoring rules, and decision thresholds.

## Solo Track: Four Lenses

### 1. Founder Constraints (6 filters)

Answers: Can I build this?

**Scoring:** Each constraint is PASS / Mixed / FAIL.
**Result:** Count the PASS results. Founder Fit = High if ≥5 PASS, Low if <5 PASS.

| Constraint | PASS means | FAIL means |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Energy, not drainage | Average day excites you; shipping, feedback loops, customer interaction | Meetings, approvals, friction consume your energy |
| 2. Build for the model curve | Product gets better as models improve; value stays in layers models enhance | Product value degrades as models improve; you're building the implementation layer |
| 3. Win and exit | 5–7 years, business is sellable or runnable without you | Business tied to your personal delivery; no clear buyer; permanent job |
| 4. Bootstrap by default | Can reach first milestone without outside capital | Requires capital before meaningful progress |
| 5. Revenue decouples from time | Revenue scales independently of hours; near-zero marginal cost per customer | Revenue tied to your time; services, hourly, FTE work |
| 6. Wealth games, not status | Building an asset; positive-sum creation | Competing for a seat; zero-sum status game |

### 2. Market Opportunity (5 dimensions, 1–5 each)

Answers: Is it worth building?

**Scoring:** Each dimension is 1–5. Composite = average of the five.
**Bands:** ≥4.0 High, 2.5–3.9 Medium, <2.5 Low.

| Dimension | 1 (weak) | 3 (moderate) | 5 (strong) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Problem Severity** | Nice-to-have; people live with it | Real pain; would consider paying | Makes customers lose money or time; urgent fix |
| **Market Size** | Hundreds worldwide | Thousands in region | Millions in region |
| **Defensibility** | Commodity; easily copied | Some switching cost or data moat | Real network effect or switching cost |
| **Timing** | Technology doesn't exist yet | Technology proven; market starting to notice | Customers actively looking; timing urgency |
| **Value Capture** | Customers won't pay even if value exists | Customers might pay if positioned right | Customers see value and pay willingly |

### 3. Runway & Sequencing (3 filters, + sequence position)

Answers: Can I start it now? Does it use my edge? Can it pay inside my runway?

**Scoring:** Each filter is PASS / Mixed / FAIL.
**Verdict:**
- SWING: all three PASS
- STRETCH: two PASS and one Mixed
- WAIT: any FAIL

**Sequence Position** (applies to BUILD ideas only):
- Current Vine: start this now
- Next Vine: start after current vine is stable
- Career Capital Builder: builds a skill you'll use later
- Not Sequenceable: no clear path to next idea

| Filter | PASS | FAIL |
|---|---|---|
| **Startable Now** | Can build MVP in 2–4 weeks; have core skills | Missing a key skill or tool; would take months to unblock |
| **Capability Leverage** | Uses skills you have or skills worth building | Requires skills outside your growth trajectory |
| **Runway Fit** | Can generate revenue inside your runway target | Needs more runway than you have, or can't be sustained on side income |

### 4. Income-Bridge Viability (3 dimensions, weighted composite)

Answers: What best extends your runway alongside the primary bet?

**Scoring:** Each dimension is 1–5.
**Composite formula:** (Time to Revenue × 0.30) + (Income Ceiling × 0.40) + (Revenue Predictability × 0.30)
**Bands:** ≥4.0 Excellent, 3.0–3.9 Viable, 2.0–2.9 Marginal, <2.0 Not Viable.
**Red flag:** Any dimension scoring 1 is flagged regardless of composite.

| Dimension (weight) | 1 (weak) | 3 (moderate) | 5 (strong) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Time to Revenue (30%)** | 6+ months to first customer | 2–4 months | <1 month; can charge immediately |
| **Income Ceiling (40%)** | <$500/month realistic | $1000–2000/month | $3000+/month |
| **Revenue Predictability (30%)** | Highly variable; depends on luck | Reasonably predictable | Repeatable process; churn understood |

## The Quadrant

Maps Founder Fit (horizontal) against Market Opportunity band (vertical).

|                  | High Market (≥4.0) | Medium (2.5–3.9) | Low (<2.5) |
|------------------|---|---|---|
| **High Fit (5+/6)** | BUILD | BUILD | PASS |
| **Low Fit (<5/6)** | OPPORTUNITY GAP | KILL | KILL |

**Verdicts:**
- **BUILD:** Founder fit is high. Build it (regardless of market band, as long as it's Medium or better).
- **OPPORTUNITY GAP:** Market is high, but a constraint fails today. Status = Watching. Record Constraint Flip Conditions; review quarterly.
- **KILL:** Low fit + low/medium market. No clear reason to build.
- **PASS:** High fit but low market. Could build it, but ceiling too small to justify the constraint slots it uses.

## Venture Track: Four Lenses

The venture track mirrors the solo track but inverts the assumptions: capital is required, full-time is expected, exit is fund-returning, and partnership is scored.

### 1. Venture Constraints (7 filters)

Answers: Can we build this as a venture?

**Scoring:** PASS / Mixed / FAIL each. Result: X/7 PASS.

Hard gates:
- **Motivation (PASS/FAIL):** Founder is compelled to do this, willing to dedicate 5+ years
- **Personal Runway (PASS/FAIL):** Founder has 12+ months of runway (or can raise salary)

Near-gates:
- **Model Curve:** AI improvements accelerate the product (PASS/Mixed/FAIL)
- **Venture-scale outcome:** Can this reach $10M+ revenue or equivalent impact? (PASS/Mixed/FAIL)

Tolerance filters:
- **Founder right-to-win:** Credible unfair advantage (PASS/Mixed/FAIL)
- **Capital efficiency:** Can reach PMF on reasonable seed capital (<$500k) (PASS/Mixed/FAIL)
- **Founder equity:** Founder has sufficient control post-raise (PASS/Mixed/FAIL)

### 2. Market Opportunity (5 dimensions, same as solo)

Adjusted for venture scale: $10M+ exit target, 3–5 year horizon.

### 3. Partnership Fit (6 weighted dimensions)

Answers: Is the co-founder the right partner?

**Scoring:** 1–5 each. Composite is weighted.
**Gate:** Strong (≥4.0) / Workable (3.0–3.9) / Fragile (2.0–2.9) / Misaligned (<2.0).

Dimensions:
- Founder skillset overlap and gap coverage
- Commitment and motivation alignment
- Equity and governance clarity
- Previous working relationship quality
- Network and fundraising access
- Investor credibility and board potential

### 4. Venture Feasibility (4 filters + Ethics/Legal veto)

Answers: Can we operationalise this venture?

**Scoring:** 4 filters, each PASS / Mixed / FAIL.
**Ethics / Legal gate:** PASS / FAIL (binary veto; FAIL is always NO-GO).
**Composite:** CLEARED / CONDITIONAL / BLOCKED.

Filters:
- **Capital path:** Viable fundraising route, realistic ask (PASS/Mixed/FAIL)
- **Go-to-market:** Credible path to first revenue inside raise runway (PASS/Mixed/FAIL)
- **Build & operate:** Team can execute the roadmap (PASS/Mixed/FAIL; non-gating)
- **Ethics / Legal:** No material ethics or legal risk (PASS/FAIL; binary veto)

## Master Verdict (Venture Track)

Combines four lenses through a six-step cascade.

| Step | Rule | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ethics / Legal = FAIL | **NO-GO** (hard stop; cannot be overridden) |
| 2 | Venture Constraints < 5/7 AND Market < 3.0 | **PASS** (not compelling enough) |
| 3 | Partnership Fit = Misaligned | **PASS** (wrong partner, regardless of other scores) |
| 4 | Market ≥ 4.0 + Partnership Fit Strong + Feasibility CLEARED | **PURSUE** (go now) |
| 5 | Market ≥ 3.5 + Partnership Fit Workable + Constraints ≥ 5/7 | **SHAPE** (plan the partnership and cap table) |
| 6 | Market ≥ 3.5 + Ethics PASS but another lens fails | **WATCH** (monitor constraints and partnership) |
| Default | | **PASS** (not yet fundable) |

## Lifecycle Statuses

### Solo Track

- **Backlog:** Captured, scored, not yet validating
- **Validating:** Customer conversations or evidence gathering in progress
- **Built:** Implementation complete but not yet validated by real user or paid signal
- **Active:** Validated; actively building/launching
- **Parked:** Viable but deprioritised; can return to prior stage
- **Watching:** High market (≥4.0) but constraint fails; review quarterly
- **Killed:** Fails constraints or evidence disproves thesis; requires post-mortem

### Venture Track (adds one status)

- **Shaping:** Verdict SHAPE; planning partnership and cap table before fundraising

**Status decay (solo):** A Validating idea with no evidence inside 14 days auto-demotes to Backlog (or fires its kill-trigger if recorded).

## Decision Gates at Idea Creation

### Solo track

1. If Quadrant = OPPORTUNITY GAP → record Constraint Flip Conditions
2. If Quadrant = KILL and Market < 4.0 → run kill ceremony at creation (unless explicitly backlogged with reason)
3. If Market ≥ 4.0 → status is Watching (not Killed or Parked)

### Venture track

1. Cascade to Master Verdict
2. If Market ≥ 4.0 + Ethics PASS but another lens fails → status is Watching
3. If Ethics FAIL → status is NO-GO (hard stop; never fund)
4. If SHAPE verdict → status is Shaping (plan before fundraising)

## Allocation Rule

At any time:
- **One primary bet** (Validating or Active solo idea, or one Active venture)
- **One income-bridge slot** (optional; ranks ideas by Income-Bridge Viability)
- **Everything else in Backlog**

The income-bridge slot must not exceed 20–30% of your time, or it becomes the primary bet.

## Quick scoring checklist

1. Read the four frameworks (or venture equivalents)
2. Score each lens honestly (not optimistically)
3. Calculate composites (simple average for Market; weighted for Income-Bridge)
4. Determine quadrant or master verdict
5. Set status and record any conditional requirements (flip conditions, post-mortem, etc.)
6. Log the decision in `decisions.md`
7. Add overview row
8. Run integrity checker to verify
