🔗Key Partners

  • App-store platforms for distribution and billing.
  • Model providers for the weekly reflective summary.
  • No durable partner moat; this is a thin layer over commodity inputs.

✅Key Activities

  • Building a lightweight tracker and the weekly auto-generated reflection.
  • Consumer growth marketing (the hard part, and not an existing edge).
  • Retention experiments to fight structural churn.

🏭Key Resources

  • The tracker app and its summary feature.
  • No defensible data layer; the gating gap is the absence of a distribution channel.

🎁Value Propositions

  • A weekly "mirror" summary of your daily routines at a glance.
  • A lightweight tracker plus an auto-generated reflection of what you actually did.
  • Weak as a proposition: habit-tracking is a nice-to-have, and the summary is trivially copied.

❤Customer Relationships

  • Self-serve direct-to-consumer subscription with a free tier.
  • Low engagement; users churn after a week or two.

🚚Channels

  • App-store search and paid acquisition.
  • Social and content, with no owned audience to start from.

👤Customer Segments

  • Individual consumers interested in self-improvement and routine-building.
  • A large but crowded category; the trackable slice is small per app.

💰Cost Structure

  • Consumer acquisition cost is the gating problem; it almost certainly exceeds lifetime value at this price.
  • Model usage for the weekly summary.
  • App development and maintenance.

💵Revenue Streams

  • $4.99/month consumer subscription with a free tier.
  • High churn and lumpy conversion cap the income ceiling.
