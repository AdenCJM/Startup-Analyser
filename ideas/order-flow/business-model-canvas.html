🔗Key Partners

  • Accounting-tool vendors whose APIs receive approved orders (the top three first).
  • Document-parsing model providers that supply the line-item extraction layer.
  • Distributor associations and ops communities as referral channels into the niche.

✅Key Activities

  • Hardening document extraction across messy supplier email and PDF formats.
  • Building and maintaining accounting-tool integrations.
  • Onboarding design-partner distributors and turning them into paid pilots.

🏭Key Resources

  • The accumulated supplier-parsing dataset (the gating asset; accuracy compounds with it).
  • Existing product and workflow-automation skill, deployed directly.
  • The shared order-queue product itself.

🎁Value Propositions

  • One approvable order queue in place of inboxes and spreadsheets.
  • Auto-extracted line items from supplier emails and PDFs, so no manual re-keying.
  • Mismatch flags on price and quantity before a wrong delivery ships.
  • Approved orders pushed straight to the buyer's accounting tool.

❤Customer Relationships

  • Embedded self-serve SaaS; the queue becomes daily operational infrastructure.
  • Hands-on onboarding for the first design partners, then a repeatable setup.

🚚Channels

  • Direct outreach to owner-operators who personally chase purchase orders.
  • Referrals through distributor ops communities.
  • Content on the cost of manual PO reconciliation.

👤Customer Segments

  • 5-50 person wholesale distributors, the underserved core.
  • The operations lead or owner-operator who owns purchasing today.
  • Expansion: the wider ops team as seat count grows.

💰Cost Structure

  • Document-parsing model usage is the gating variable cost; it scales with order volume.
  • Integration build and maintenance across accounting tools.
  • Product engineering and early-stage founder-led sales and support.
  • Near-zero marginal cost per added distributor once integrations exist.

💵Revenue Streams

  • Per-seat SaaS subscription at $79 per seat per month, billed annually.
  • Most distributors start with two to four seats and expand as the ops team adopts it.
  • Planned supplier-portal upsell as a second revenue line.
