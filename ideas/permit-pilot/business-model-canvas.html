🔗Key Partners

  • Councils and permitting bodies whose requirements the rules data tracks.
  • Builder associations and trade bodies as referral channels.
  • Document-generation and model providers for the submission-pack assembly.

✅Key Activities

  • Per-jurisdiction rules research and maintenance (the draining surface area).
  • Building the guided intake and submission-pack generator.
  • Nailing one jurisdiction end to end, then templating the rules-capture process.

🏭Key Resources

  • The curated, maintained per-jurisdiction rules dataset (the moat and the gating asset).
  • The guided-intake and pack-assembly product.
  • Regulatory-domain knowledge built up over time.

🎁Value Propositions

  • A ready-to-submit permit pack in place of a maze of council-specific forms.
  • Plain-language intake that maps the project to the right jurisdiction's requirements.
  • Fewer rejected applications, so fewer weeks lost at the bottom of the pile.
  • A live tracker of what is still outstanding.

❤Customer Relationships

  • Mixed transactional and subscription: per-application use plus an unlimited plan for active builders.
  • Guided, assisted intake rather than pure self-serve in the early jurisdictions.

🚚Channels

  • Direct outreach to small residential builders who lodge a few applications a month.
  • Referrals through builder associations and trade bodies.
  • Content on permit-delay cost and council-specific requirements.

👤Customer Segments

  • Small residential builders lodging a handful of permit applications a month.
  • Owner-operators and project managers who own the application today.

💰Cost Structure

  • Per-jurisdiction rules research and upkeep is the gating cost; coverage economics decide whether it scales.
  • Document-generation and model usage per pack.
  • Product engineering and early founder-led sales.
  • Scoping for regulatory liability if a generated pack is wrong.

💵Revenue Streams

  • $149 per application, or $399/month unlimited for active builders.
  • Mix of recurring seats and lumpier per-application revenue early on.
  • Path to scale: template the rules-capture process to add jurisdictions faster.
