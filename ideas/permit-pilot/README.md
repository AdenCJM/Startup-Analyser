# PermitPilot -- A guided assistant that walks small builders through council building-permit applications and assembles the submission pack for them.

**Status:** Watching  **Type:** Standalone idea  **Track:** solo  **Created:** 2026-01-15  **Evidence Confidence:** scored-from-priors

> Fictional example idea. It shows the Opportunity Gap case: a strong market that fails a founder constraint, so the status is Watching and Constraint Flip Conditions are tracked. Replace it with your own.

## Elevator Pitch

Building permits stall small builders for weeks because the application pack is a maze of council-specific forms. PermitPilot asks plain-language questions, fills the right forms for the right council, and hands back a ready-to-submit pack.

## The Buyer

Owner-operators and project managers at small residential builders who lodge a handful of permit applications a month.

## The Problem (In Their Words)

> "Every council wants something slightly different and one missing form sends the whole application back to the bottom of the pile."

## The Offer

A guided intake that maps the project to the correct jurisdiction's requirements, generates the submission pack, and tracks what is still outstanding.

## Price Point

$149 per application, or $399/month unlimited for active builders.

## Revenue Model

Per-application and subscription mix. Path to scale: nail one jurisdiction end to end, then template the rules-capture process to add jurisdictions faster.

## Key Risks

- Per-jurisdiction rules drift; stale requirements produce rejected applications and kill trust.
- The moat (maintained rules data) is also the cost centre; coverage economics decide whether it scales.
- Regulatory liability if a generated pack is wrong needs clear scoping.
