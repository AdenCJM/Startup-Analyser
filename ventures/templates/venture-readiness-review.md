**Trigger:** the first review after the first co-founder term discussion, OR 3+ logged co-founder conversations, whichever comes first.

## Raise Status

- Investor conversations live since the last review: who, stage, temperature.
- Term-sheet proximity: any soft circles, lead interest, committed capital?
- Target raise size and dilution vs the Capital Plan: still aligned, or has the market repriced the round?
- Non-dilutive capital in play (grants, accelerators)?
- Decision: the raise thesis is holding / needs reshaping / stalled.

## Co-founder Terms

- **Role split:** agreed vs open. Who owns product, commercial, capital, and the domain?
- **Equity & vesting:** is the split agreed? Is the vesting schedule and cliff agreed? Acceleration on change-of-control?
- **Control & board:** board composition, voting, tie-breaks. Decided or deferred?
- **Thesis alignment:** are you and the co-founder converged on the same venture, or two adjacent ones?
- **Walk-away conditions:** what ends the partnership cleanly, and is it written down before the leap?
- **Partnership-opportunity decay date:** by when must this be locked before the co-founder takes another partner or another idea? {date}
- Decision: terms converging / need to validate the co-founder's load-bearing claims / diverging.

## Right-to-Win and Edge

- **Combined edge:** is the product of both founders' stacks still a real, differentiating advantage on this specific venture, or has the market caught up?
- **Validation of claims:** have the co-founder's load-bearing claims (investor readiness, reputation, track record) been confirmed from primary sources since the last review? Mark any still-unvalidated claim provisional.

## Go-to-Market / Regulatory Pipeline

- Live customer conversations: who, what stage, what they would need to see next.
- Wedge contracts: any quoted, scoped, or signed since the last review?
- Scale positioning: any movement toward the larger budget where the scale revenue lives?
- Regulatory / certification path: any change to the licensing, data/privacy, or certification burden and its cost or timeline?
- Time-to-first-revenue: has it moved in or out?

## Ethics / Legal Posture

- Has the capability drifted toward any use-case that fails the Ethics / Legal gate?
- Re-affirm the line: state plainly what this venture will and will not build.
- If a PASS (conditional) commitment is live, is its signature deadline still in date?
- Decision: the line holds / drift detected, correct or stop.
