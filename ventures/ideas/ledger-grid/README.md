# LedgerGrid -- a horizontal data-lineage and audit layer for enterprise AI pipelines

**Status:** Shaping  **Type:** Standalone venture  **Track:** venture  **Created:** 2026-01-15  **Master Verdict:** SHAPE

> Venture track. The solo bootstrapped frameworks invert here: capital is required not avoided, the leap is full-time not side-built, the target is a fund-returning exit not a personal income target, and the partnership is a first-class scored axis. This is a fictional worked example used to demonstrate the venture lenses end to end. Edit or delete it.

## Elevator Pitch

As enterprises wire AI into core workflows, they cannot answer a basic auditor question: where did this output come from? LedgerGrid is the lineage and audit layer that records the full provenance of every model input and output, so any answer can be traced and proven.

## The Buyer

The head of platform or data governance at a large enterprise deploying AI in regulated or high-stakes workflows.

## The Problem (In Their Words)

> "We ship AI features faster than we can explain them, and the first hard question in any audit is one we cannot answer."

## The Offer

A drop-in lineage layer that instruments existing AI pipelines, captures provenance end to end, and exposes an audit-ready trace for any output.

## Price Point

Usage-based platform pricing, enterprise contracts starting around six figures annually.

## Revenue Model

Recurring usage-based platform revenue; land with one regulated business unit, expand across the enterprise.

## Master Verdict

Apply the six-step cascade strictly in order.

1. **Ethics/Legal veto:** PASS -> not NO-GO.
2. **Constraint gates:** no hard-gate FAIL, no unrecoverable near-gate FAIL -> continue.
3. **Primary 2x2:** High fit (6/7) x HIGH market (4.4) -> provisional PURSUE.
4. **Partnership modulation:** Workable -> hold and flag (no demotion on its own).
5. **Equity-protection modulator:** Partnership > Equity & Commitment Alignment unresolved (3) AND Venture-Scale sub-check (b) soft (Constraint 3 Mixed) -> cap at SHAPE ("fix the cap table first").
6. **Feasibility overlay:** CONDITIONAL -> cap at SHAPE. Market >=4.0 already satisfied; no change.
