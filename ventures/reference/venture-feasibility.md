# Venture Feasibility Framework (Venture Track)

**Purpose.** Evaluate whether a venture can get *funded*, get *legal*, get *sold*, and get *built*, within the founders' ethical line. This framework replaces the solo track's Runway & Sequencing and Income-Bridge frameworks, which are irrelevant under the venture condition (VC-backed, with a co-founder, full-time from day one, no moonlighting and no salary to protect).

This sits alongside three other lenses on the venture track.

- **Venture Constraints**: does this fit the founders, the fund-returning ambition, and the full-time leap?
- **Market Opportunity (venture-scale)**: is this worth building, as a venture-scale market bet?
- **Partnership Fit**: is this the right idea to build *with this co-founder*, on these terms?
- **Venture Feasibility** (this doc): can it get funded, legal, sold, and built, within the founders' ethical line?

The four lenses are integrated by the **Master Verdict** in Part 2.

> **Make it yours.** The filters, the composite rules, and the cascade are an editable default. The two deterministic rules below (the gating-Mixed rule and the equity-protection modulator) are the load-bearing logic; keep them or replace them deliberately, but do not lose the discipline of evaluating the gating filters first.

---

# Part 1 - The Four Feasibility Filters

Ventures die for reasons that are easy to wave away in a pitch: the capital plan does not survive contact with a real cap table, a regulatory regime makes the product unshippable at a manageable cost, the first contract never closes, or the work crosses a line the founders will not cross. These filters test exactly those failure modes.

Score each filter **PASS / Mixed / FAIL**, with one exception: the **Ethics / Legal Line** is binary **PASS / FAIL** and acts as a **veto** over the whole evaluation.

The original venture framework carried a fifth filter for the founders' regulatory standing. Its only domain-neutral content (whether the team can mechanically hold the operational position the work requires) folds into Build & Operate Feasibility below.

---

## Filter 1 - Capital Path (gating)

Does the capital *plan* survive contact? Venture Constraints already judged whether this venture is fundable in principle; this filter judges whether the *specific path* is sound: which fund leads the seed, what milestone de-risks the next round, whether there is a runway buffer if growth stalls, and whether non-dilutive capital can extend the line.

A sound plan names: (a) a credible lead for the seed given the team's network; (b) an 18-24 month milestone that visibly de-risks the next round (a signed contract, a funded pilot converting, a regulatory approval); (c) a runway buffer that survives a slow patch; and (d) any non-dilutive overlay (grants, accelerators) the venture is eligible for.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| PASS | A named, credible seed lead; a clear 18-24 month de-risking milestone; a runway buffer that survives one slow cycle; a non-dilutive overlay identified and eligible. |
| Mixed | A plausible lead and milestone, but one leg is soft: the buffer is thin, the de-risking milestone depends on a contract not yet in hand, or non-dilutive eligibility is unconfirmed. |
| FAIL | No credible lead given the network; no milestone that de-risks the next round; no buffer for a slow patch; a capital plan that assumes revenue on a timeline the market cannot deliver. |

**Ask:** If the next round slips six months, does the company survive, and what was the named milestone that was supposed to unlock it?

*(Gating filter. With Go-to-Market / Regulatory Pathway, the most common venture-killer.)*

---

## Filter 2 - Go-to-Market / Regulatory Pathway (gating)

Is there a real, walkable route to first revenue and then to scale, AND can the venture clear whatever regulatory, compliance, or certification burden its market imposes, at a manageable cost?

A sound pathway names: (a) the *first* contract or instrument that produces revenue or a funded pilot inside roughly 12 months; (b) a named first customer, not just a buying office; (c) a credible bridge from pilot to durable revenue inside runway; and (d) any licensing, data or privacy regime, or industry certification the market requires, with a manageable cost and timeline to clear it.

This filter combines the route-to-revenue question with the shipping-cost reading of regulation: "this regulation creates a moat" is an *upside* (score it in Market Opportunity > Defensibility); "we need this licence or this certification before we can ship a single unit" is a *cost and a gate* (score it here). The gap between a promising pilot and durable revenue, where many early ventures stall, is treated here as ordinary go-to-market and scale risk.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| PASS | A first instrument identified and winnable in under 12 months; a named first customer with a felt problem; a credible pilot-to-revenue bridge that closes inside runway; and any regulatory or certification burden is clear, walkable, and affordable. |
| Mixed | A plausible first instrument and customer, but the bridge to durable revenue is unproven or lands just outside runway, **or** a licence, permit, or certification is on the critical path and adds material time or cost before first ship. The risk is live but mitigable. |
| FAIL | No instrument produces revenue or a funded pilot in under 12 months; no named first customer; the only path to durable revenue sits far beyond runway; or a regulatory or certification regime structurally blocks the target market on any realistic timeline. |

**Ask:** Who signs the first cheque, on what instrument, by when, what carries the company from pilot to durable revenue before the money runs out, and what must you be licensed or certified to do before you can ship?

*(Gating filter. With Capital Path, the most common venture-killer.)*

---

## Filter 3 - Build & Operate Feasibility (non-gating)

Can the team actually build and operate this? Two parts: the technical core is achievable with the founding team (or a credibly hireable early team), and the venture can hold the operational position the work requires (it can stand up the people, infrastructure, and compliance posture the market demands).

This filter absorbs the residue of the old founder-standing filter: the mechanical question of whether the venture can hold the position it needs to occupy. It does **not** re-score whether the team's edge is an *advantage*; that is scored once, in Partnership Fit > Combined Founder-Market Fit. Here we only ask whether the build and the operating posture are mechanically achievable.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| PASS | The technical core is achievable with the founding team or a credibly hireable early team, and the venture can stand up the operational posture (people, infrastructure, compliance) the work requires. |
| Mixed | The core is achievable but carries a real execution risk, or a piece of the operating posture (a key hire, an infrastructure dependency, a compliance build) is uncertain but addressable. |
| FAIL | The technical core is beyond what the team can realistically build or hire for inside runway, or the venture has no path to stand up the operational posture the work requires. |

**Ask:** Can this team build the core and stand up the operating posture the work requires, inside the funded runway, independent of whether the edge is an advantage?

---

## Filter 4 - Ethics / Legal Line (binary veto)

**Binary PASS / FAIL. A FAIL forces the Master Verdict to NO-GO and is never Watching; a veto cannot be flipped.**

Does the venture clear a basic ethics and legal check? It must be lawful, it must not cross a line the founders have recorded as a won't-build, and it must not trip the explicit exclusions of the investors backing it (LP or ESG mandates). The founders' won't-build line is recorded once and applied consistently.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| PASS | The work is clearly lawful and within the founders' recorded line; no investor-exclusion trigger. |
| FAIL | The work is unlawful, crosses the founders' recorded won't-build line, trips an investor exclusion, or is a silent conditional-yes with no written, signed, deadlined commitment on record. |

**Three recordable Ethics states.** To stop a near-the-line idea oscillating between a silent FAIL and an over-stated PASS, the Ethics result takes one of three recordable states. The filter is still scored against the veto cascade as PASS (clean or conditional-met) vs FAIL; the conditional state is a PASS sub-type with a tracked obligation, not a fourth band.

| State | Meaning | Cascade treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **PASS** (clean) | Clearly lawful and within the founders' recorded line; no investor-exclusion trigger. | PASS, no veto. |
| **PASS (conditional)** | Near a line the founders care about, but a written mitigation or commitment is **recorded with a named signature deadline** in the README and `decisions.md` (Track: venture), and the idea is **only advanced after that commitment lands**. (Generic example: a data-privacy commitment, or a dual-use commitment.) | PASS while the obligation is live; logged in the Ethics register. If the deadline lapses unsigned, it reverts to **FAIL → NO-GO**. |
| **FAIL** | Unlawful, crosses the recorded won't-build line, trips an investor exclusion, **or is a silent conditional-yes with no written, signed, deadlined commitment on record.** | Veto → NO-GO (terminal, never Watching). |

A **silent conditional-yes remains a FAIL.** The only thing that earns the PASS (conditional) state is a named deadline plus a recorded commitment.

**Ask:** Is the work lawful, does it cross the founders' recorded won't-build line, does it trip any investor exclusion, and if it is near a line, is the commitment written, signed (or deadlined for signature), and logged?

## Composite Assessment

The four filters resolve to one composite: **CLEARED / CONDITIONAL / BLOCKED.** Ethics is a veto; Capital Path and Go-to-Market / Regulatory Pathway are the two **gating** filters.

| Composite | Rule |
|---|---|
| **BLOCKED** | Ethics / Legal = FAIL (veto), **or** FAIL on *both* gating filters (Capital Path and Go-to-Market / Regulatory Pathway). |
| **CONDITIONAL** | Not BLOCKED, and **any one of**: a Mixed-or-worse on **either gating filter**; **or** >= 3 Mixed across the filters; **or** one mitigable non-veto FAIL. |
| **CLEARED** | Not BLOCKED, both gating filters at PASS, <= 2 Mixed **on non-gating filters only**, and no FAIL. |

**The gating-Mixed rule.** A **Mixed (or worse) on EITHER gating filter forces CONDITIONAL regardless of total Mixed count.** CLEARED requires both gating filters at PASS. This makes the composite deterministic: evaluate the gating filters first, then count non-gating Mixed.

**Ethics logging rule.** Every Ethics result that is a FAIL, or a PASS (conditional), records the specific won't-build line or the conditional commitment in **both** the idea README **and** `decisions.md` (Track: venture). The ethics line is a permanent record, not a per-evaluation note.

**Feasibility feeds the verdict; it does not set it.** CLEARED applies no change in the Master Verdict; CONDITIONAL caps the verdict at SHAPE; BLOCKED (non-ethics) routes to WATCH if Market >= 4.0 else PASS; BLOCKED (ethics veto) forces NO-GO. See Part 2, cascade step 6.

# Part 2 - The Master Verdict

The Master Verdict is the authoritative integration of all four lenses. It produces one of five labels and one lifecycle status.

## Four-Lens Input Table

| Lens | Produces | Feeds the verdict by |
|---|---|---|
| Venture Constraints | PASS / Mixed / FAIL per constraint; gates applied (5 Motivation, 7 Runway hard gates; 2 Model Curve, 3 Venture-Scale Outcome near-gates) | The founder-fit High/Low axis of the primary 2x2 (step 3); gate routing (step 2); the equity-protection modulator (step 5) |
| Market Opportunity | 1-5 per dimension; simple-average composite; band HIGH / MED / LOW | The market-band axis of the primary 2x2 (step 3); the >= 4.0 override (step 6); the Market Size <= 2 scale flag (step 6) |
| Partnership Fit | 1-5 weighted composite; gate band Strong / Workable / Fragile / Misaligned | Partnership modulation (step 4); the equity-protection modulator via Partnership > Equity (step 5) |
| Venture Feasibility | Four filters; composite CLEARED / CONDITIONAL / BLOCKED; the Ethics veto | The feasibility overlay (step 6); the Ethics veto (step 1) |

## Primary 2x2 (founder-fit × Market band)

Gates from cascade step 2 are already applied before this 2x2 is read. There is **no LIFESTYLE quadrant**: a good-fit-but-sub-scale idea is a **PASS** on the venture track and routes back to the solo track.

```
                     High Founder Fit            Low Founder Fit
                     (gates pass, strong fit)    (weak fit, gates pass)
                 +---------------------------+---------------------------+
Market HIGH      |                           |                           |
(>= 4.0)         |          PURSUE           |          SHAPE            |
                 |  Right founders, real     |  Real market, founder     |
                 |  market: build it.        |  fit not yet there:       |
                 |                           |  shape it into the bet.   |
                 +---------------------------+---------------------------+
Market MED       |                           |                           |
(2.5 - 3.9)      |          SHAPE            |          PASS             |
                 |  Good fit, market needs   |  Weak on both live axes.  |
                 |  sharpening: shape it.    |  Pass (the Market >= 4.0  |
                 |                           |  override may rescue to   |
                 +---------------------------+  WATCH, step 6).          |
Market LOW       |          PASS            |          PASS             |
(< 2.5)          |  Good founders, no        |  Dead on both axes.       |
                 |  venture-scale market:    |                           |
                 |  route to the solo track. |                           |
                 +---------------------------+---------------------------+
```

The 2x2 yields a **provisional** PURSUE / SHAPE / PASS. The modulators in steps 4-6 then hold or lower it, and in one case may promote a borderline SHAPE to PURSUE.

## The Five Verdict Labels

| Label | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **PURSUE** | Right founders, real venture-scale market, feasible, partnership holds. The bet. | Build. At most one venture idea is ever **Active** at a time. |
| **SHAPE** | The idea is live but one lens needs fixing first: fit, market sharpness, a feasibility condition, the cap table, or the partnership. | Shape it: run the specific fix, then re-verdict. The dominant early co-founding state, status **Shaping**. |
| **WATCH** | Routed to PASS on fit or feasibility, but Market >= 4.0 and Ethics PASS. The market is real; the founders are not ready. | Watch: document Flip Conditions, monitor signals. The highest-Market WATCH = Crown Jewel. |
| **PASS** | Good-fit-but-sub-scale, or weak on the live axes. Not a venture-track bet. | Pass on the venture track; route a good-fit sub-scale idea back to the solo track. |
| **NO-GO** | An Ethics / Legal veto or a (non-ethics) Feasibility BLOCKED. Terminal. A labelled sub-type of PASS so the reason stays visible. | Log the won't-build line (for an ethics veto) in the README and `decisions.md` (Track: venture). Never Watching; a veto cannot be flipped. |

## The Cascade (apply strictly in order)

1. **Ethics / Legal veto.** Ethics / Legal = FAIL -> **NO-GO** (terminal; never Watching; log the won't-build line). NO-GO is a labelled sub-type of PASS so the reason stays visible.
2. **Constraint gates.** If a hard gate (5 Motivation, 7 Runway) FAILs, or a near-gate (2 Model Curve, 3 Venture-Scale Outcome) is unrecoverable -> route toward **PASS**, UNLESS Market >= 4.0 and Ethics PASS -> **WATCH** (+ Flip Conditions).
3. **Primary 2x2.** Founder fit (High/Low, gates already applied) × Market band -> provisional **PURSUE / SHAPE / PASS**. There is NO LIFESTYLE quadrant; a sub-scale market on the venture track is PASS (good-fit-but-sub-scale ideas route back to the solo track).
4. **Partnership modulation.** Strong = hold (may promote a borderline SHAPE -> PURSUE); Workable = hold + flag; Fragile = cap at **SHAPE** ("fix the terms first"); Misaligned = cap at **SHAPE** and flag a re-team. Partnership NEVER produces a kill.
5. **Equity-protection modulator.** Fires when **Partnership > Equity & Commitment Alignment is unresolved (score <= 3, that is, terms live or not yet agreed or worse)** **AND** **(the founder's Venture-Scale sub-check (b) is soft, that is, Constraint 3 personal slice Mixed-or-worse, OR personal runway is Mixed-or-worse, that is, Constraint 7 Mixed-or-worse)**. When it fires -> **cap the verdict at SHAPE** ("fix the cap table first"). The modulator is a hold-or-lower only; it never kills and never promotes. It depends only on the founder's own scores.
6. **Feasibility overlay + Market override.** CLEARED = no change; CONDITIONAL = cap at **SHAPE**; BLOCKED (non-ethics) = **WATCH** if Market >= 4.0 else **PASS**. Then anything otherwise PASS but Market >= 4.0 with Ethics PASS -> **WATCH** (+ Flip Conditions); the highest-Market WATCH = Crown Jewel. Surface a Market Size <= 2 scale flag regardless of composite.

## Lifecycle Status Mapping

The venture track uses the existing lifecycle statuses plus one new status, **Shaping.**

| Status | When it applies |
|---|---|
| **Backlog** | Logged, not yet evaluated against the four lenses. |
| **Validating** | Under active evidence-gathering against a specific open question. |
| **Active** | The single venture currently being built. **At most one venture idea is ever Active.** |
| **Shaping** (new) | The verdict is SHAPE: the idea is live and being actively shaped (fit, market, a feasibility condition, the cap table, or the partnership) before it can become Active. |
| **Parked** | Set aside deliberately; not killed, not Watching. |
| **Watching** | PASS on fit or feasibility but Market >= 4.0 and Ethics PASS; Flip Conditions logged. |
| **Killed** | Dead on the venture track. NO-GO (ethics veto) records the won't-build line and stays visible as a labelled sub-type of PASS. |

**Why Shaping is new.** On the venture track the SHAPE verdict is the dominant, active early co-founding state: the founders are working a real idea with a real market and fixing the one lens that is not ready yet (sharpening the market, resolving the cap table, closing a feasibility condition, or repairing the partnership). None of the other statuses captures this. It is not Backlog (it is being worked), not Validating (the work is shaping, not just gathering evidence), not Active (it is not yet the one venture being built), and not Parked or Watching (it is live, not set aside). Shaping fills that gap.
